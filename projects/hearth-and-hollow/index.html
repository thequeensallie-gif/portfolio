The project

A café, a bookstore, and a place to belong.

Hearth & Hollow is a conceptual independent café and bookstore. The brief was to build a complete visual identity that could make one cohesive, welcoming world across the brand’s touchpoints.

My role

Concept development
Visual identity
Brand applications

Deliverables

Logo system, packaging, signage, merchandise, customer journey, and brand direction

Tools

Adobe Photoshop
Adobe Illustrator

The opportunity

More than a quick coffee stop.

Specialty cafés can feel interchangeable, while independent bookshops compete with the ease of online shopping. The concept responds to people looking for a place with personality: somewhere to read, work, meet a friend, or stay a little longer.

The direction

Let coffee and stories share a home.

I brought a neighborhood café and a curated bookstore together under one identity. Warm color, natural textures, elegant type, and a botanical coffee-cup mark give the concept a calm, storybook quality.

Designed around a feeling

A welcoming place for different ways to unwind.

The concept personas helped shape a space that could work for a quiet afternoon alone or a relaxed evening with friends.

01

Concept persona

From inspiration to identity

A little warmth, a little wonder.

The visual direction brings together natural materials, warm light, books, pastries, and quiet corners.

The identity system

A mark that feels like a warm welcome.

The final logo pairs a steaming cup with botanical details, surrounded by the Hearth & Hollow name. I developed black-and-white and color versions for flexible use.

Color palette

Forest greens, coffee browns, soft neutrals, and a small note of gold build the grounded, welcoming palette.

Made to live beyond the logo

What I learned

Design can make people feel at home before they ever walk through the door.

This project helped me explore how research, sketching, typography, color, and a flexible logo system can work together to express comfort and connection. I especially enjoyed carrying one clear idea across many different brand applications.
