Projects/Move Free

✦ Inclusive fitness · Community campaign concept

Movement for everyone

Move
Free

Move with purpose. Give with impact.

A community fitness event concept that makes room for different bodies, abilities, and ways of moving—while supporting accessibility and inclusion.

My roleBrand identity & digital campaign design
DeliverablesResponsive landing pages · Journey map · Social campaign
✧✦

01 · The opportunity

Movement can look different for everyone.

Many fitness events are built around a narrow idea of exercise and competition. Move Free responds with an inclusive community fundraiser that welcomes people across ages and abilities, and raises awareness and support for accessibility programs.

The campaign needed to communicate the event clearly, feel energetic, and make inclusion visible across web and social. I developed a visual identity and a digital experience that bring those goals together.

02 · Many ways to take part

Run. Walk. Ride. Roll.

Choice is part of the invitation. The campaign frames movement as personal, shared, and open to different paces and abilities.

Run 3K · 5KWalk Choose your paceRide CyclingRoll Wheelchair racing

03 · Designed around participation

Make the next step feel clear.

Rebecca Coleman, a community-minded wellness advocate, helped center the experience on someone who values accessibility and meaningful social impact.

01

Before the event

Discover the campaign, understand the options, and find practical event details without friction.

Clear dates, locations, registration choices, and activity information.
02

At the event

Feel welcomed, choose a comfortable way to participate, and connect with the community.

Visible wayfinding, supportive activities, and an inclusive tone.

04 · The visual system

Bright energy, grounded in clarity.

Cyan, teal, purple, indigo, and yellow give the campaign a lively, celestial feel. A consistent type scale, simple icons, and strong calls to action help people scan and act.

01

Use legible type, direct labels, and clear calls to action so the event is easy to understand.

02

Celebrate every pace

03

Connect action to impact

Keep the community purpose visible: movement supports local wellness and disability advocacy.

05 · Across screens and streets

A campaign people can follow.

The identity moves from a print poster to a responsive event landing page and social campaign. Event details shown in the visuals belong to the original project concept.

06 · What I learned

Consistency has to flex with the experience.

This project challenged me to carry a visual identity across different formats without losing clarity. Adapting the landing page for mobile helped me think more carefully about hierarchy, information, and how someone moves through an experience on a smaller screen.

It also gave me room to design a campaign around inclusion and community impact, with a clear invitation for people to participate in the way that works for them.
