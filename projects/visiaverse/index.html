Dreams Unravel Here.

✧✦

01 · The experience

A ride that feels like stepping into a dream.

Dreamweaver’s Paradox is a concept attraction within VisiaVerse, a futuristic theme park built around imagination and nonlinear storytelling. The ride moves guests through shifting environments inspired by lucid dreams and time loops.

The design challenge was to create a visual identity that felt mysterious and otherworldly while still helping guests understand where to go and what to expect. I developed the attraction’s brand, guest journey, touchpoints, and print and digital campaign direction.

02 · Who it welcomes

Three ways to enter the story.

The experience had to feel exciting to thrill-seekers, rich in story for fantasy fans, and clear and approachable for families.

01

The thrill seeker

Looks for high-intensity attractions, striking visuals, and something worth sharing.

Needs bold cues, a quick read, and clear wayfinding.
02

The fantasy lover

Enjoys immersive worlds, atmospheric details, and stories that unfold as they explore.

Needs a cohesive world with lore and memorable motifs.
03

The family explorer

Plans an outing for different ages and wants a magical experience that feels easy to navigate.

Needs legible information and an inviting, approachable tone.

03 · The visual system

Celestial details, dreamlike contrast.

The identity pairs expressive display lettering with crescent moons, stars, and a deep violet palette, then uses clear supporting type to keep practical information readable.

01

Build atmosphere

Deep violets, dark tones, and luminous accents set a surreal, immersive mood.

02

Give the eye an anchor

Moon and star marks create a repeatable visual language across attraction touchpoints.

03

Keep the journey clear

Wayfinding and key messages need strong hierarchy, even inside a world that feels unpredictable.

04 · From first glimpse to final scene

A visual thread through the guest journey.

01

Discover

Posters, digital screens, and park maps spark curiosity.

02

Approach

Land markers and wayfinding introduce the world.

03

Anticipate

Queue graphics and story details build suspense.

04

Experience

Ride visuals carry the dreamlike atmosphere through the attraction.

05

Remember

Exit moments and photo touchpoints invite guests to revisit the story.

05 · Campaign direction

Enter the dream.

The campaign carries the attraction’s atmosphere into simple, recognizable invitations across digital and print placements.

What this project taught me

Making the magic work across the whole experience.

This project helped me connect research, storytelling, visual identity, and practical information. I learned to think beyond a single logo and consider how a brand guides someone from discovering an attraction to stepping into its world.

Balancing atmosphere with clear wayfinding became a central design challenge. The finished identity and campaign direction show how one visual language can support both imagination and the guest experience.
